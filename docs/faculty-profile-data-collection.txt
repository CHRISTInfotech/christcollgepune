FACULTY PROFILE PAGE — INFORMATION TO COLLECT FROM EACH EMPLOYEE
(Reference design: https://lavasa.christuniversity.in/computer-science/faculty-details/)

Send this list to each faculty member and ask them to fill in whatever applies.
Leave anything blank if it doesn't apply to them — blank sections simply won't
show up on their profile page. Do not paraphrase their answers; use their exact
wording/titles as given.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BASIC DETAILS (needed for every faculty member, shown at the top of the page)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Full Name (with title, e.g. Dr./Mr./Ms./Fr.)
- Designation (e.g. Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, HOD, Professor)
- Department
- Qualification(s) (e.g. MCA, PhD, M.Com, NET/SET — comma separated)
- Specialization / Area of Expertise
- Official Email ID
- Contact Number (optional — confirm they're OK making this public)
- Photograph (passport-size, plain background, JPG/PNG, min 400x400px)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- A short written bio in their own words (3–6 sentences): current role, subjects
  taught, research interests, and any notable contribution to the department.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ARTICLES IN JOURNALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For EACH published article:
- Title of the article
- Journal name
- Year of publication
- Volume / Issue number (if available)
- ISSN / DOI (if available)
- Indexing (UGC-CARE / Scopus / Web of Science / Peer-reviewed / etc.)
- Co-author names (if any)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. BOOK CHAPTERS / ARTICLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For EACH book chapter or article in an edited book:
- Chapter/article title
- Book title
- Editor(s) / Publisher name
- Year of publication
- ISBN
- Co-author names (if any)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PARTICIPATION IN CONFERENCES / SEMINARS / SYMPOSIUM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For EACH conference/seminar/symposium attended or presented at:
- Title of paper presented (or "Attended" if no paper)
- Name of the conference/seminar/symposium
- Organizing institution
- Dates (From – To)
- Level (National / International)
- Mode (Online / Offline), if relevant

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. INVITED TALKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For EACH invited talk / guest lecture delivered:
- Topic of the talk
- Host institution / organization
- Date
- Audience/occasion (optional, e.g. "FDP for junior faculty")

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PATENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For EACH patent:
- Title of the invention
- Patent application/registration number
- Status (Filed / Published / Granted)
- Inventor names
- Year
- Field of invention

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. RESEARCH PROJECTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For EACH research project (ongoing or completed):
- Title of the project
- Funding/sponsoring agency
- Role (Principal Investigator / Co-Investigator / Team member)
- Duration (start – end, or "Ongoing")
- Grant amount (optional)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. WORKSHOPS / FDP / TRAINING PROGRAMMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For EACH workshop / Faculty Development Programme / training attended or conducted:
- Title of the workshop/FDP/training
- Organizing institution
- Dates (From – To)
- Role (Participant / Organizer / Resource Person / Trainer)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. AWARDS / ACHIEVEMENTS / OTHERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For EACH award/achievement:
- Name/title of the award or achievement
- Awarding body/organization
- Year
- Brief reason/context (optional, one line)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. CONSULTANCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For EACH consultancy assignment:
- Organization/client name
- Nature of the consultancy work (brief description)
- Duration (From – To)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES FOR WHOEVER COLLECTS THIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Collect a filled copy of this file (or a Google Form/Sheet with the same
  fields) per faculty member.
- Photo should be sent separately as an actual image file, not pasted into text.
- Most faculty will only fill in Basic Details + Profile — that's fine; the rest
  are optional per-entry lists and should only appear if they have real content.
